\section{Midpoint Assessment}
Our project now studies M1 DMP exploitation through both Augury and GoFetch. The completed experimental effort has focused on Augury activation and reverse engineering; the completed existence sweep reports activation at 12 of 14 sampled training sizes, including all sampled sizes from 32 upward. The largest median speedup among activating offsets is 16.3$\times$ at 256 training pointers. These results support Augury-style DMP activation on our M1, while the eight-pointer anomaly, scheduling controls, and missing raw sweep data limit claims about precise thresholds and prefetch distance. The remaining work connects that characterization to exploit demonstrations and to controlled Asahi Linux and M4 comparisons. This progression will let us distinguish what we reproduced, what changed across platforms, and what remains unexplained without overstating the preliminary evidence.
